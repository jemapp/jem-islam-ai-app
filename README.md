# Jem Islam AI App
An all-in-one Islamic companion featuring AI Hadith & Tafsir search in 4 languages (English, Arabic, Amharic, Afan Oromo), live Qibla compass, Hijri calendar, prayer times, audio recitation, and digital Tasbih.

## Project Summary
- **Category:** mobile
- **Design Style:** modern-dark
- **Target Platform:** responsive-web
- **Stack:** html-js
- **Features:** Local Storage, Sound Effects, Offline PWA Support

---

## 📱 How to Run on iPhone Safari (No Mac or Computer Required!)
1. Unzip this downloaded archive on your iPhone using the built-in **Files** app.
2. Open `index.html` directly in **Safari**.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
4. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
5. You now have a standalone, full-screen native-like mobile app icon on your iPhone home screen!

---
